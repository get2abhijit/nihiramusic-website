## Question: are German Terms & Conditions needed?
No.
- Terms & Conditions are not legally required for this website in India or Germany: it sells nothing, has no
  accounts and makes no contracts with visitors. The new "Terms of Use" are optional; English is sufficient.
- Imprint (Impressum, § 5 DDG): applies to providers established in Germany, and possibly to foreign providers
  whose site is aimed at Germany (then commentators expect the § 5 content in German). An Indian LLP with an
  English, India-focused site is most likely not aimed at Germany. A complete English imprint labelled
  "Impressum" is kept as low-cost insurance. Add German labels/version only if the site starts targeting
  Germany (German concerts, tickets, German-language content).
- Privacy policy: GDPR reaches non-EU operators only when they offer services to or monitor people in the EU;
  this site does neither in a strict sense. An accurate English notice is still provided. India's DPDP Act
  2023 / DPDP Rules 2025 come into force in phases (13/14 Nov 2025, Nov 2026, May 2027).
